01 / ABOUT ME

A clinical perspective with an analytical mindset.

I am a radiation therapist pursuing advanced education and clinical training in medical dosimetry. My professional development is grounded in direct patient care, radiation oncology workflow, treatment delivery, and collaboration with physicians, physicists, dosimetrists, therapists, and other members of the cancer care team.

As I move toward dosimetry, my focus is shifting from delivering an established treatment plan to understanding how the plan is constructed, optimized, evaluated, and adapted to the clinical objective. I am especially interested in the intersection of technical precision, clinical judgment, and patient-centered care.
